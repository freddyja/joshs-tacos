# Josh's Tacos App — Quick Guide v001 (DRAFT, app not built)
1. Open the app link. Tap EN/ES to switch language.
2. Tap menu items or combos to add them. Adjust in the cart. The subtotal is an estimate.
3. Optional: tick "Ask about the Maria Special".
4. Tap "Text my order". Your messages app opens with the order filled in. Review it, add your name, and press Send yourself. You can also tap Call or Copy order.
5. Josh's Tacos replies to confirm the price, pickup time, and availability. Pickup only. Cash and electronic payments accepted.
- Share: sends the app link to a friend.
- Install: adds the app to your home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
- Check for updates: gets the latest menu and prices. Your cart is kept.
